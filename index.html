Possible Projects

So many ways to add meaning to people's lives.

There are so many ways to add meaning to people's lives with technology — here are some examples of projects we have done.

Memorials and Honor Sites

judyrosenthal.com is a memorial website for Judy Rosenthal, an incredible artist. The website honors her family, plays into her love for genealogy and cooking, and tells the story of her life through the people she loved and the children's books she wrote.

Visit the site →

Websites for Nonprofits

eefoodpantry.org is a website for the Ecumenical Emergency Food Pantry to get word out to their clientele, have statistics to show for their donors, and be a resource for people looking for food pantries near them.

Visit the site →

Websites for Small Businesses

The shops and small businesses around us keep our community running. A good website helps people find them, understand what they do, and walk through the door.

Technology to Help Other Hackley Clubz

Every club at Hackley has something it wishes it could do. We build the features that make those things possible — and connect our school in the process.

About Us

Why we do this.

Hackley Tech Angels empowers people excited about technology to solve problems that can help their community or the broader world.

Joining Hackley Tech Angels also gives its members the same experience that they would need at a company or startup. You will be building things that real people depend on.

Real projects

Work that ships, gets used, and stays online after you are done with it.

Real users

Families, nonprofits, small businesses, and clubs who count on what you make.

Startup-style teamwork

Meeting a client, scoping a problem, splitting the work, and shipping together.
